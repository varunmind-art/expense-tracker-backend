## 1. What This App Does

Think of this app as a smart notebook for your money. It helps you:

- 💸 **Record every expense** — from a ₹10 chai to a ₹50,000 rent payment
- 🏦 **Track your savings** — mutual funds, emergency fund, stocks
- 💰 **Log your income** — salary, freelance, dividends
- 📊 **See the whole picture** — beautiful charts on one dashboard
- 🎯 **Set budgets** — so you don't overspend
- 🔄 **Automate recurring items** — rent, SIPs, subscriptions
- 📧 **Auto-import receipts** from your Gmail inbox
- 🏪 **Track merchants** — see where your money actually goes

You can use it on your **laptop**, **tablet**, or **phone** — everything stays
in sync automatically.

## 2. Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Open the app in your browser.
2. Click **Register** (or **Create Account**).
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** — your name (or nickname)
   - **Email** — any email address
   - **Password** — something only you know
4. Click **Register**.

✨ **Magic moment:** The moment you register, the app automatically creates
10 starter categories for you — Food & Dining, Transport, Shopping, Rent,
Salary, and so on. You can edit or delete them later.

### Logging In

1. Enter your **email** and **password**.
2. Click **Login**.
3. You'll land on your **Dashboard**.

### Forgot Your Password?

1. On the login page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email.
3. Check your inbox for a reset link.
4. Click the link, set a new password, and log in.

## 3. The Dashboard — Your Money at a Glance

The Dashboard is the first thing you see after logging in. It's like the
dashboard of a car — everything important at a glance.

### Top Section — Monthly Flow

This tells you at a glance:

- **Spent** — how much you've spent this month
- **Saved** — how much you've put into savings
- **Unspent** — money still in your pocket
- **Total Income** — everything that came in
- **Savings Rate** — the percentage of income you saved

The **colour ring** on the right changes colour:
- 🔴 **Red** — saving less than 20% (try to save more!)
- 🟡 **Yellow** — saving 20–40% (good progress)
- 🟢 **Green** — saving 40%+ (excellent!)

### Change the Time Period

At the top right, choose:
- **This Week** — last 7 days
- **This Month** — the current month (default)
- **Last Month** — the previous month

Every chart below updates automatically.

### Summary Cards

Three quick numbers:
- **Total Spent** — sum of all expenses
- **Transactions** — how many entries
- **Categories Used** — how many categories

### Charts

- **Expenses by Category (pie)** — a colourful circle showing where your money went
- **Spending Trend (line)** — a graph of your daily spending
- **Top Spending Categories** — your biggest categories, ranked
- **Top Merchants** — the shops you've visited most. **Click any merchant** to
  see the last transactions, category breakdown, and frequency.

### Budget Progress

If you've set budgets, you'll see progress bars:
- 🟢 **Green** — under 70% of the budget used
- 🟡 **Yellow** — 70–90% used
- 🔴 **Red** — over 90% used (careful!)

### Savings Breakdown

If you've logged savings, a colourful donut shows where you've saved —
mutual funds, emergency fund, and so on.

## 4. Tracking Your Expenses

An "expense" is money you spend. Chai, petrol, clothes, movie tickets — all
expenses.

### How to Add an Expense

1. Click **+ Add** (top right of Dashboard, or on the Expenses page).
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Type** — leave as **💰 Expense** (there's a toggle at the top; you'll
     switch it for savings)
   - **Amount** — how much you spent (e.g., 250)
   - **Date** — when (defaults to today)
   - **Category** — pick one (e.g., Food & Dining)
   - **Merchant** — optional, but useful! Start typing or pick from the dropdown.
   - **Note** — optional (e.g., "Chai with Ravi")
3. Click **Save**.

That's it! The expense appears on your Dashboard instantly.

### Editing an Expense

1. Go to the **Expenses** page.
2. Find the entry.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Change anything and click **Save**.

### Deleting an Expense

1. Find the entry.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Confirm.

### Filtering & Searching

At the top of the Expenses page, you can:

- **Search** — type anything: "Swiggy", "chai", "petrol" — matches merchant,
  category, or note.
- **Filter by Category** — show only Food, for example.
- **Filter by Merchant** — show only Swiggy transactions.
- **Tab filter** — All / 💰 Expenses / 🏦 Savings.
- **Sort** — by Date, Amount, Category, or Merchant.
- **Sort order** — click the ↑/↓ button.

### Exporting to Excel/Sheets

Click **Export CSV** at the top. You'll download a file that opens in Excel
or Google Sheets — perfect for backups or sharing with your accountant.

The CSV includes: Date, Type, Category, Merchant, Amount, Note, Receipt.

## 5. Tracking Your Savings

Savings are money you **keep aside** — mutual funds, emergency fund, stocks,
fixed deposits. It's different from spending.

### How to Add Savings

Savings use the same form as expenses, with one extra step:

1. Click **+ Add**.
2. In the form, click the toggle at the top: **🏦 Savings**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Amount** — how much you saved
   - **Category** — Mutual Funds, Emergency Fund, etc.
   - **Note** — optional (e.g., "Monthly SIP")
4. Click **Save**.

### Where Savings Show Up

- **Dashboard → Monthly Flow Card** — the "Saved" figure
- **Dashboard → Savings Rate Ring** — % of income saved
- **Dashboard → Savings Breakdown** — donut chart by category
- **Expenses page** — with the 🏦 Savings tab

### Pro Tip: Make a Category a "Savings" Category

If you have a category like "Investments" that's always a savings item:

1. Go to **Categories**.
2. Click **Edit** on that category.
3. Set the **Type** to **🏦 Savings**.
4. Save.

Now whenever you pick that category, the form auto-sets the type to **Savings**.

## 8. Merchants — Who You Paid

A "merchant" is any shop, website, or person you paid. Swiggy, Amazon,
BigBasket, your landlord — all merchants.

### Why Track Merchants?

Because you might be spending ₹8,000/month on Food — but is it groceries
or is it Swiggy? Merchant tracking reveals the truth.

### Adding a Merchant to an Expense

When adding an expense:

1. Find the **Merchant** field.
2. Choose from the dropdown.
3. Or click **+ Add new merchant…** to create a new one on the spot.

### Managing Your Merchant List

Go to **Merchants** in the navbar. At the top, you'll see:

**Manage Regular Merchants** — your curated list.

- **Add** — type a name and click **+ Add**
- **Rename** — click ✎ next to a merchant
- **Deactivate** — click ◐ (hides it from the dropdown without deleting)
- **Delete** — click ✕
- **Import** — click **⬇ Import from existing expenses** to auto-add every
  merchant you've already used

### Merchant Aliases — One Name, Many Versions

Suppose you have transactions from "Amazon", "AMZN", and "amazon.in" — the
app treats them as 3 different merchants by default. **Aliases** fix that.

1. Find the canonical merchant (e.g., "Amazon") in the list.
2. Click **+ alias**.
3. Type "AMZN" and click **Add**.
4. Repeat for "amazon.in".

Now when you add an expense with "AMZN", it automatically rolls up under
"Amazon" — no duplicate names in your stats.

### Merchant Insights

Below the Manage panel, you'll see a table of every merchant, showing:

- **Primary Category** — what you mostly buy from them
- **This Month** spent
- **Visits** — transaction count
- **Avg** — average per visit
- **All Time** — lifetime spend
- **Trend** — ▲ Up / ▼ Down / ★ New / — Flat vs last month

**Click any row** to open a side drawer with 3 tabs:

- **Transactions** — last 20 entries with this merchant
- **Categories** — a breakdown of what you buy from them
- **Frequency** — a heatmap showing *when* you shop there (day of week,
  week of month, hour of day). Also shows your "busiest day" as a fun insight.

### Filtering Expenses by Merchant

On the Expenses page, use the **All Merchants** dropdown. Or click the
**Filter** link on any merchant row to auto-navigate.

## 10. Recurring Rules — Set It and Forget It

Some expenses and savings happen **on a schedule** — rent on the 1st of
every month, a SIP on the 5th, Netflix on the 15th. You don't want to
manually log these every time.

**Recurring Rules** handle this automatically.

### Creating a Recurring Rule

1. Go to **Recurring** in the navbar.
2. Fill in:
   - **Description** — e.g., "Monthly Rent"
   - **Amount** — e.g., 25000
   - **Category** — Rent (Expense) or Mutual Funds (Savings)
   - **Frequency** — Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Yearly
   - **Day of Month or Day of Week** — depending on frequency
3. Click **Create Rule**.

### Example Rules

| Description | Category | Frequency | Day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly Rent | Rent | Monthly | 1 |
| SIP — Axis Bluechip | Mutual Funds | Monthly | 5 |
| Netflix | Entertainment | Monthly | 15 |
| Gym Membership | Healthcare | Monthly | 10 |
| Milk Delivery | Groceries | Daily | — |

### How It Works

Every night at **12:05 AM IST**, the app checks for due rules and
automatically creates the expense or savings entry. You'll see a note like
"Monthly Rent (Auto - Recurring)" on the entry.

### Pausing a Rule

Click **Pause** on any rule. It'll stop firing until you reactivate it.
Useful for temporary holds.

### Editing a Rule

Click **Edit** to change the amount, category, frequency, or day.

## 11. Gmail Integration — Auto-Magic Receipts

Instead of manually adding every receipt, let the app read your inbox and
import them for you.

### Setting It Up (One-Time)

1. Go to **Settings** in the navbar.
2. Click **Connect Gmail**.
3. A popup opens asking permission — click **Allow**.
4. You'll see: **"Gmail is connected"** ✅

That's it. From now on, the app scans your Gmail once every 6 hours.

### What Gets Imported

The app recognises receipts from:

- **Amazon** and **Amazon Pay**
- **Swiggy**, **Zomato**, **Uber**
- **Flipkart**
- **ICICI Bank** credit card alerts
- **YES BANK** credit card alerts (these go to Pending for review — see below)

### Syncing Manually

Want results instantly? Go to **Settings** → **Sync Now**. The app will
scan Gmail right away.

### Where Imported Expenses Go

- **Amazon, Swiggy, ICICI, etc.** → auto-created as expenses in the
  **"Other"** category (you can change them later)
- **YES BANK alerts** → go to the **Pending** queue for your review

### What Gets Ignored

The app skips emails that aren't real expenses: cashback, refunds, order
confirmations, delivery alerts, etc. Only genuine payments get recorded.

## 12. Pending Transactions — Your Review Queue

Some emails (like YES BANK alerts) don't follow a perfectly consistent
format, so the app asks you to review them before turning them into
expenses.

### Viewing the Queue

Go to **Pending** in the navbar (or check the notification if you have
any). You'll see a list of transactions the app has extracted.

### For Each Pending Item You Can:

- **Edit** — fix the amount, merchant, date, or category
- **Reject** — delete it (not a real expense)
- **Confirm** — turn it into a real expense. Pick a **category** from the
  dropdown, then click Confirm.

### Why This Matters

Once confirmed, the item becomes a normal expense and appears everywhere —
Dashboard, Expenses, Merchant stats, etc.

## 17. FAQ

**Q: Do I have to fill in every field?**
A: No — only **Amount** and **Category** are required. Everything else is
optional but helps you get better insights.

**Q: What's the difference between an "Expense" and "Savings"?**
A: Expense = money gone (chai, rent, petrol). Savings = money kept
(mutual funds, emergency fund). Both matter, but they're tracked separately
so your Savings Rate is accurate.

**Q: Can I edit an entry after saving it?**
A: Yes! Every entry has an **Edit** button. Nothing is permanent.

**Q: Will the app work offline?**
A: Partially — you can view the app, but you need internet to add or sync
entries.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes — all data is encrypted and stored securely in the cloud. Only you
can access it with your password.

**Q: What happens if I delete a category?**
A: Existing expenses keep the category name for record-keeping, but the
category won't appear in future dropdowns. Default categories can't be
deleted.

**Q: Why are some Gmail imports going to Pending?**
A: Some bank alerts (like YES BANK) don't follow a perfectly consistent
format. The app extracts what it can and asks you to confirm — this
prevents wrong data from entering your records.

**Q: Can I use the same account on multiple devices?**
A: Absolutely — that's the best part! Log in anywhere, and see the same
data.

**Q: What happens to my recurring SIP if I skip a month?**
A: It keeps running unless you **Pause** it. Pausing is a good idea when
you travel or skip a month.

**Q: Can I share this with my family?**
A: The app is designed for a single user. Shared access is on the roadmap
for a future update.

**Q: How do I see my spending from 6 months ago?**
A: On the Dashboard, use **Last Month** filter, or on the Merchants page
use the month picker. For full history, use **Export CSV**.
